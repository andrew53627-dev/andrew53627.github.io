Our purpose

Curiosity takes flight here.

The Aerial Drone Club is a welcoming place to explore unmanned aircraft systems through hands-on learning. We build practical skills while promoting creativity, teamwork, aviation safety, and responsible drone use.

Whether you are brand new to drones or already eager to learn more, there is a place for you in the club.

What we explore

Hands-on learning, from first flight to future tech.

01

Fly safely

Learn safe flight practices, drone regulations, and responsible decision-making.

02

Know your drone

Build confidence with controls, maintenance, and basic troubleshooting.

03

Try aerial photography and videography with a creative point of view.

04

Tackle flight challenges, mapping activities, and mission planning.

05

Build what is next

Explore engineering, coding, and emerging drone technology together.

Club highlights
