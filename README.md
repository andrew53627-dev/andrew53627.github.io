# East Chapel Hill Aerial Drone Club

Welcome to the website for the East Chapel Hill High School Aerial Drone Club. We are a student-led community for learners who are excited about drones, flight, photography, engineering, and responsible innovation.

## Our Purpose

The Aerial Drone Club gives students a welcoming place to explore unmanned aircraft systems through hands-on learning. Our goal is to build practical skills while promoting creativity, teamwork, aviation safety, and responsible drone use.

Members can learn about:

- Safe flight practices and drone regulations
- Drone controls, maintenance, and basic troubleshooting
- Aerial photography and videography
- Flight challenges, mapping, and mission planning
- Engineering, coding, and emerging drone technology

## How to Join

All East Chapel Hill High School students are welcome—no previous drone experience is required.

1. Watch for club meeting announcements and attend an introductory meeting.
2. Meet the club officers or faculty advisor to complete the membership sign-up.
3. Review and agree to our flight-safety expectations.
4. Bring your curiosity and participate in activities, workshops, and events.

Students do not need to own a drone to join. Club equipment and beginner-friendly guidance are available during meetings and approved activities.

## Our Accomplishments

We are proud of the work our members have done to grow a safe, creative drone community at East Chapel Hill High School. Highlights include:

- Creating a student-led space for learning about aerial drone technology
- Hosting hands-on flight practice and safety workshops
- Developing skills in aerial photography, videography, and drone operations
- Collaborating on flight challenges and technology projects
- Encouraging responsible, safety-first drone use across our school community

## Get Involved

Whether you want to fly, film, build, code, or simply learn how drones work, there is a place for you in the Aerial Drone Club. Join us to take your ideas higher.

`REPLACE-WITH-CLUB-EMAIL` in `index.html` with the club or faculty advisor email
address.
